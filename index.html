Pay the least interest — allocation optimizer

Smith Maneuver

Models re-borrowing your mortgage's principal paydown from the HELOC and investing it — the core Smith Maneuver mechanic. This is a projection based on assumptions you set below, not a guarantee, and isn't tax or investment advice.

Assumes your mortgage is on a readvanceable product (like your CIBC Home Power Plan) where HELOC room increases as the mortgage is paid down, that HELOC interest is paid out of pocket each period rather than added to the loan, and that the deductibility of that interest holds up under CRA's rules for your situation — worth confirming with an accountant before relying on the tax-refund number.
